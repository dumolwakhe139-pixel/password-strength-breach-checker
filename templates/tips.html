Tips for strong passwords

  • Use 12 or more characters. Length matters most.
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols.
  • Avoid names, dates and words such as "password" or "qwerty".
  • Use a different password for every account.
  • Consider a passphrase of several unrelated words.
  • Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication.
